Adjust grind size, water temperature, or brew ratio based on what you taste in the cup.

⚡ Under-Extracted (Sour / Salty)

Water passed through the coffee bed too quickly or wasn't hot enough to dissolve sugars and balancing oils.

  • • Grind finer (increases surface area)
  • • Increase water temperature (+2°C)
  • • Extend brew contact time or agitate bloom
✨ Sweet Spot (Balanced & Syrupy)

18%–22% extraction yield. Bright fruit acids are harmonized by caramelized sugars and a clean, lingering finish.

  • • Vibrant clarity & distinct origin notes
  • • Smooth sweetness without dryness
  • • Record your winning recipe in Passport!
🔥 Over-Extracted (Bitter / Astringent)

Excessive extraction pulled dry plant tannins and woody compounds from the coffee solids.

  • • Grind coarser (speeds up drawdown)
  • • Lower water temperature (especially for dark roasts)
  • • Use a slightly shorter ratio (e.g., 1:15 instead of 1:17)

Your data stays on your device

Cherry to Cup has no accounts and no server. Your Bean Passport entries, photos, custom brewing recipes and language choice are saved only in your own browser (local storage). We do not collect, upload or sell this information.

What we collect

Nothing. This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not ask for personal information. Bean photos are resized and stored only inside your browser.

Third-party services

To load, this page fetches fonts from Google Fonts, and code libraries and map data from public content delivery networks (Tailwind CSS, cdnjs and jsDelivr). Those providers may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, under their own privacy policies.

Your control

You can edit or delete any bean or recipe at any time. Clearing your browser's site data permanently removes everything saved here. Data is not backed up, so keep your own copy of anything important.

Changes to this policy
